# JCE Figure Contract

## Shared delivery contract

- Journal: Journal of Clinical Epidemiology.
- Backend: persisted Python; matplotlib only for drawing, preview, export, and visual QA.
- Output: 183 mm page width; editable-text PDF and 300 dpi PNG; white background; Arial 7–9 pt; restrained blue, orange, neutral, and status colors.
- Display cap: two figures and six tables in the manuscript body. This package creates only Figures 1 and 2.
- Sources: versioned v2 analysis outputs and the shared v2 audit code/policy. Source data are exported with row references and SHA-256 hashes.
- Quantitative data are never simulated, imputed, or re-estimated by the plotting script. Missing source intervals remain point-only.

## Figure 1: score-specific risk grid

**Claim.** Observed in-hospital mortality risk and the never-primary minus primary difference vary across supported integer score levels in eICU-CRD and MIMIC-IV.

**Archetype.** Quantitative 2 × 2 grid. Rows are database; columns are sepsis and pneumonia.

**Panels.** Each cell contains (top to bottom): observed risks by diagnosis position, score-specific difference `δ_s`, and stacked admission-record counts. The top panel uses source risk estimates and group-specific 95% intervals only where the source marks them computed. The middle panel uses `δ_s = risk_never,s − risk_primary,s` in percentage points and its source interval. A hollow `δ_s` marker and light score band mean the computed `δ_s` interval includes zero. A diamond means the source provides the point estimate but no interval. The bottom strip stacks never-primary and primary admission records.

**Data mapping.** `level` → integer score; `risk_never` / `risk_primary` → observed risks in percent; `risk_*_ci_pp_lo/hi` → source-issued group-risk interval bounds in percent; `diff_pp` → `δ_s` in percentage points; `delta_ci_pp_lo/hi` → source-issued `δ_s` interval in percentage points; `n_never` / `n_primary` → admission records. `n` is checked against the two group counts. Any computed interval without finite bounds, noncomputed interval with source bounds, or unsupported group-risk CI schema stops the figure as a source conflict.

**Uncertainty.** Per-level intervals come from the shared v2 audit's patient-cluster calculations. Risk intervals are emitted separately by group and score. `δ_s` intervals are direct score-specific risk-difference intervals. No interval is generated by the plotting script. These are distinct from the weighted summary `Δ_w`, which is not plotted here.

**Data scope.** All rows for the four requested database-condition cells are retained. No score level is filtered or downsampled. The source CSV retains the source data row for every plotted score level.

**Reuse decision.** Figure 1 is a structural adaptation of the existing score-grid design: the panel map is retained, while the source schema and interval mapping are rebuilt for v2. Figure 2 is built anew because its reportability branches must match the v2 policy and exact thresholds.

## Figure 2: audit reportability flow

**Claim.** The shared v2 audit separates failure to produce a point estimate from failure to emit an interval.

**Archetype.** Compact process diagram. Arrows represent software decision flow, not causes of mortality or clinical actions.

**Flow.** Input contract → complete-case analysis set → common score support → deterministic record, patient, and outcome-variation checks → patient bootstrap → reportability state. Invalid required input or no common support yields no point. Loss of prespecified target support retains an observed-support point estimate and withholds the target interval. Failed deterministic checks or fewer than 20 usable bootstrap replicates retain a point estimate and withhold intervals. Passing all gates emits the point estimate and interval outputs.

**Frozen policy values.** At least 20 admission records per score level; at least 10 per score-by-definition cell; at least 20 distinct patients overall and per score-by-definition cell; nondegenerate binary outcomes in every target cell; 60 patient-cluster bootstrap replicates, at least 20 usable, and at least 10 events per definition per usable replicate. A usable replicate must retain all score levels.

**Evidence source.** The shared v2 audit implementation, the frozen v2 policy protocol/configuration, and its focused regression cases. The displayed rules are procedural checks; no causal arrows or causal interpretation are shown.
